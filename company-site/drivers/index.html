BEFORE YOU APPLY

A few useful
answers.

Where is BESA based?

Our operational base is Aylesford, Kent. BESA will confirm the relevant starting location with you during the initial conversation.

What is the daily rate?

The daily rate is £140.40, plus VAT where applicable. The team will confirm the payment and working arrangements with you before you proceed.

Are these self-employed opportunities?

Yes. We are hiring self-employed delivery drivers based in Aylesford, Kent. The team will explain the working arrangements and full terms before you proceed.

Do I need my own van?

No. BESA provides a modern 2026 automatic van for your delivery work at no cost. There is no van hire charge.

Do I pay for vehicle insurance or fuel?

No. BESA covers vehicle insurance and the fuel used for your delivery routes. Fuel cards are provided for your delivery work.

Is training paid?

Yes. BESA provides paid training to get you started, followed by coaching and support from the operations team.

What support is available?

You’ll join a friendly team with approachable managers, day-to-day operations support and ongoing coaching. Free accountancy benefits are included in the driver offer.

What licence, experience and availability do I need?

The BESA team will discuss the current role requirements, licence eligibility, driving experience and availability with you. Register your interest or ask us on WhatsApp to discuss your circumstances.

Should I send my documents now?

No. Start with the contact form. If you are invited to proceed, BESA will send a private onboarding link for the required documents and checks.
